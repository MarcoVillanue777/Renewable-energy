WHY RENEWABLE ENERGY?

The future doesn't have to depend on fossil fuels.

Renewable energy comes from naturally replenished sources such as sunlight, wind, and moving water. These technologies can help reduce greenhouse-gas emissions while providing cleaner ways to produce electricity.
