The Mechanics of Clean Energy

Understanding how renewable sources combat climate change by eliminating greenhouse emissions.

☀️ Solar Power

Photovoltaic cells capture sunlight and convert it directly into electricity at the atomic level, generating clean power with zero emissions.

💨 Wind Energy

Massive kinetic wind turbines harness atmospheric air currents, spinning generators to feed clean electrical grids globally.

💧 Hydroelectric Power

Controlled gravitational water flow forces massive internal turbines to spin, generating highly reliable, baseline renewable electricity.
